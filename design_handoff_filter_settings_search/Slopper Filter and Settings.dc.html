2Redesign directions — filter · settings · search

Shared fix across all of them: no stock Material chips, sliders or text fields. Every control is rebuilt from the vocabulary the main screens already use — Surface #11151C on 1px Border, 6px radius, uppercase mono section labels, JetBrains Mono for every value. Accent stays restrained: selection state (12% fill + accent border + accent label) and the one primary action. Saved presets appear in all three filter directions.

Checked against the agreed handoff in design_handoff_slopper_spine/: icons come from its icons.jsx set, sheet and hub structure from spine-flows.jsx / spine-settings.jsx, including its "Save view" footer action. Two deliberate departures: the handoff's Apply carries a live count ("Apply · 218 results") — dropped because you asked to keep commit-on-Apply with no live preview; and 2e rejects hub + drill-down on purpose, as the flat counter-proposal.
